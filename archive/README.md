# Archive: superseded versions

Everything in this folder is superseded by version 9 of the paper in the repository root. It is kept so that the record of what was claimed stays complete.

**Do not rely on the results reported here.** Version 9 (Section 2) withdraws the following:

- the description of I = C × G × S as a theorem (`theorem.txt`, version 7);
- the reported improvements of 44 to 52 percent and the multiplicative effects of 5.6× to 12.9× (`README_2025.md`, versions 1 to 7), together with the protocol, rubric and replication guide built on them;
- the claim that external work validates the framework (version 7);
- the claims about safety constraints (version 7).
